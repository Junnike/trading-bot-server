How the numbers are calculated

Each lamp is split into several small emitters across its real size. Every emitter sends photons in a cone: a Lambertian (120°) spread for bare LED boards and bars, a tighter cone for reflectors, or the exact beam from an uploaded IES or LDT photometric file. PPFD at a point is the sum over all emitters of output × beam shape × cosine of the incidence angle ÷ distance², the same inverse-square geometry used by DIALux and AGi32. Walls add mirror images of every lamp scaled by the wall reflectance, which is how reflective tents and white rooms gain light at the edges. DLI = PPFD × hours × 0.0036. Spectrum bands are per-lamp photon fractions (blue 400–500, green 500–600, red 600–700, far-red 700–750 nm); far-red counts in ePAR but not in PPFD. Lamp presets use typical 2025–2026 catalogue values; enter your own lamp's PPF from its spec sheet for exact numbers. The maintenance factor (default 0.9) allows for lamp ageing and dust.
