# SERVER_T11 (10.10.2026) - calibration scan of ALL settled markets, Kalshi + Polymarket. Public data only, no keys, no orders, nothing live.
Question: outside crypto candles, do cheap (longshot) or expensive (favourite) prices win more often than the price says, AFTER fees, in any category? (politics, weather, sports, economic data releases, finance ranges, other)

Respect rate limits, cache to data/, no re-pull. 3) If a category/venue cannot be pulled: UNMEASURED, do not fill gaps.
Method (no look-ahead): unit = one market. Entry price = BUY-side price (ask, or first trade after the time point if no quote history; state which) at fixed fractions of market life: 10%, 50%, 90% before close. Never use final/last price. Buckets by entry price: 0-5,5-10,10-20,20-30,30-50,50-70,70-90,90-95,95-100 c. Per category x bucket: n markets, win rate, mean price, EV c/contract = win rate - price - fee. KILL per cell: dead if n<200 or z<threshold or <60% months positive or negative in holdout; also dead if EV after fee < 1 c/contract.
Confounders to state BEFORE verdict: entry-price definition (ask vs trade), fee formula, category assignment method, min-volume filter, number of cells tested, period covered per venue, survivorship (delisted/voided markets).
Output: ONE table <=20 lines: category | venue | bucket | n | win rate | mean price | EV c/contract | z | months+ | holdout EV. Then verdict: ALIVE cells (if any) with USD/day ceiling = EV x contracts/day available at that price (state volume source or UNMEASURED).
